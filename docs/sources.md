# Sources and implementation notes

## Audit methods

| Implemented feature | Source and scope | Implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loss-based ROC AUC | Hanley and McNeil give the ranking interpretation of ROC AUC. The toolkit uses negative loss and gives half credit to ties. [The meaning and use of ROC AUC, 1982](https://doi.org/10.1148/RADIOLOGY.143.1.7063747) | [ROC and AUC](../unlearning_audit/metrics.py) |
| Low false-positive operating points | Carlini et al. motivate reporting performance at low FPR. The empirical FPR cap, inclusive calibration rule and separate holdout measurement are this project's protocol. [Membership Inference Attacks From First Principles, 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.03570) | [ROC operating points](../unlearning_audit/metrics.py) · [Independent calibration](../unlearning_audit/calibration.py) |
| Paired AUC uncertainty | Robin et al. describe paired ROC comparisons with stratified bootstrap resampling. This toolkit uses percentile intervals for paired AUC differences and reuses loss ranks to calculate the AUC of each resample. [pROC, 2011](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2105-12-77) | [Paired bootstrap](../unlearning_audit/metrics.py) |
| Retraining reference and repeat studies | The NeurIPS unlearning challenge motivates comparison with retraining. The NeurIPS checklist recommends documenting which sources of variation a statistic captures. Our fixed-split study reports descriptive dispersion across training seeds. [Challenge report, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09073) · [NeurIPS Paper Checklist](https://neurips.cc/public/guides/PaperChecklist) | [Training repeats](../unlearning_audit/study.py) · [Digits experiment](../examples/digits_demo.py) |
| Paired loss gaps | The project's ridge control motivates reporting the mean absolute difference in loss on the same rows and the same loss scale. The control preserves loss ranks while changing their magnitudes. [Experiment and derivation](ridge-controls.md) | [Loss gaps](../unlearning_audit/metrics.py) · [Ridge controls](../examples/ridge_control.py) |
| Holdout sensitivity | Khan et al. show how split leakage and scorer validity affect interpretation in multimodal unlearning. Our controlled ridge example changes holdout difficulty after fitting and uses the retrained model as a negative control for training membership. [Validity-Gated Evaluation, preprint v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02206v3) · [Experimental protocol](holdout-sensitivity.md) | [Holdout sensitivity](../examples/holdout_sensitivity.py) |
| Input fingerprints and replay | SHA-256 is specified by NIST and supplied by Python's standard library. The canonical record format and replay checks are defined by this project. [Secure Hash Standard](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/180-4/upd1/final) · [Python hashlib](https://docs.python.org/3/library/hashlib.html) | [Fingerprints](../unlearning_audit/fingerprints.py) · [Replay](../unlearning_audit/verification.py) |
| Accessible chart output | W3C guidance motivates accompanying complex charts with a description and an equivalent view of the data. The toolkit provides named SVG charts, tables and a full ROC CSV export. [W3C Complex Images](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/complex/) | [Report and CSV rendering](../unlearning_audit/report.py) · [Study rendering](../unlearning_audit/study.py) |

## Ridge experiments

- Cao and Yang describe machine unlearning through additive statistics, including linear regression. The ridge demonstration applies the same algebraic principle to its own fixed-feature regularized objective, subtracting the removed rows' contributions to the Gram matrix and feature-target cross product. [Towards Making Systems Forget with Machine Unlearning, 2015](https://doi.org/10.1109/SP.2015.35) · [Control code](../examples/ridge_control.py)
- Björck's treatment of least squares explains the numerical sensitivity of normal equations. The benchmark compares subtracting sufficient statistics, rebuilding the normal equations, and independently refitting with scikit-learn's SVD solver. The stress matrix, tolerance, timing protocol and reported measurements are specific to this repository. [Numerical Methods for Least Squares Problems](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/abs/10.1137/1.9781611977950.ch2) · [scikit-learn Ridge](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.linear_model.Ridge.html) · [Benchmark protocol](ridge-benchmark.md) · [Benchmark code](../examples/ridge_benchmark.py)
- The experimental block QR demonstration uses a stacked factorization of augmented data inspired by tall-skinny QR. The sequential prototype caches factors and recomputes blocks affected by each request. Its numerical tests establish agreement with a retained-data SVD on small examples. [Demmel et al., communication-optimal QR](https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.2664) · [Block QR experiment](../examples/block_ridge.py) · [Sequential experiment](sequential-experiment.md)
- Sequential requests motivate tracking errors over the whole deletion path. Shang et al. study error accumulation for different second-order methods and privacy settings. The ridge policy experiment is a numerical comparison with its own fixed thresholds and does not implement their certification method. [Shang et al., 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03445) · [Policy experiment](../examples/reconstruction_policies.py)

## Related evaluation protocols

- Ribero, Schrab and Gretton compare candidate, original and retrained output distributions with a relative three-sample test. Their experiment uses repeated model fits. A test on one aligned export would need a design that accounts for shared examples and defines which training randomness is sampled. [AISTATS 2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v300/ribero26a.html)
- Tu, Hu and Ma formalize an unlearning sample inference game. The game's metric and the fixed-model AUC used here answer different questions. [NeurIPS 2025](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/beb290ec14261c16b9269a256b55f029-Abstract-Conference.html)
- An OpenUnlearning issue reports difficulty reproducing published scores from a released checkpoint. The toolkit's replay command starts from exported losses. Reproducing the training and scoring pipeline also requires the source data, checkpoints, configuration and evaluation code. [OpenUnlearning issue #199](https://github.com/locuslab/open-unlearning/issues/199)

## Example data and software

- Alpaydin and Kaynak's UCI handwritten digits dataset is supplied by scikit-learn's `load_digits`. The examples remove exact duplicate feature rows and scale the pixels. The SGD demo predicts digit labels, and the ridge benchmark derives a parity target. [UCI dataset](https://doi.org/10.24432/C50P49) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [scikit-learn data loader](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.datasets.load_digits.html)
- The CPU classifier uses scikit-learn's `SGDClassifier.partial_fit`. The ridge examples use scikit-learn for independent SVD refits and NumPy for sufficient statistics and linear solves. The demo environment pins its direct and transitive dependencies. [SGDClassifier API](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.linear_model.SGDClassifier.html) · [Pinned demo dependencies](../examples/requirements-demo.txt)
